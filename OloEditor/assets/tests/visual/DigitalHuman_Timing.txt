NOT A BENCHMARK: this box also hosts CI runners for another repo, so the absolute
figures move. The two arms are captured back to back, each after a DISCARDED

switch forces. An earlier version of this file timed the first capture after
that switch and reported a uniform +95% that was the rebuild, not the skin.

HOW TO READ A NEGATIVE NUMBER. The full stack cannot really be cheaper than the
split-only control. Where the percentage comes out negative, or flips sign
between runs, the honest reading is that THE COST OF THE SKIN STACK IS BELOW
THIS FIXTURE'S NOISE FLOOR at 384x384 with one head on screen — not that it is
free. A real per-pass budget needs GPU timer queries on a quiet box and a scene
with enough skin in it to dominate the frame. What this file can resolve, and
therefore all it claims, is that the stack does not cost an order of magnitude.
