# Advisors, automation and onboarding

Status: consolidated v1.0 draft for repository adoption, 2026-11-27. Approved directions below are full-game design unless explicitly scoped; candidate algorithms, exact values and validation plans are not approved implementation mandates.

## Design contract

Advisors have recognizable personalities and responsibilities. They can disagree about investment priorities or risk appetite, never about the underlying facts. Explain causes and options, allow suppression and configurable interruption. Effective policy inheritance and overrides must be visible.

The tutorial must hook players immediately through building and opening a resort, then withdraw guidance; it is fully optional. Provide contextual teaching and a searchable encyclopedia. Generate factual values from authoritative definitions; reviewed prose and tested examples reduce documentation drift. Avoid long mandatory tutorials or simplified gameplay masquerading as accessibility.
