# Progression, research and personal mastery

Status: consolidated v1.0 draft for repository adoption, 2026-11-27. Approved directions below are full-game design unless explicitly scoped; candidate algorithms, exact values and validation plans are not approved implementation mandates.

## Design contract

Progression emerges from physical construction, money, knowledge, geography and mastery, not generic XP or resort levels. Research should unlock new capabilities; historical availability limits technology, with plausible early adoption and manufacturer participation. Basic usability and automation are not research rewards. Later technologies can be horizontal alternatives; older infrastructure remains viable where appropriate.

Mature resorts can be stable and profitable without artificial crises. Landmarks, events, heritage, optional contracts and new regions provide self-directed goals. The finite historical tech tree ends without ending the save; era-locking and speculative future content remain open. No mandatory expansion, account-level advantages or artificial retention mechanics. Persistent multi-project management is deferred; ordinary blueprints remain supported.

**Disposition:** Accepted.

### P-24

**Disposition:** Accepted direction.

<a id="aud-09"></a>

### AUD-09

**Disposition:** Accepted direction.

Research unlocks new gameplay capabilities rather than withholding basic usability or routine automation. Manufacturer development partnerships and historically plausible early adoption are supported. Research budget, expertise progression and event frequency remain experimental; avoid passive waiting, repetitive node selection and asset-spam grinding.

## Validation and acceptance (proposed test focus)

Test research investment versus waiting, manufacturer trials, technology relevance, viable small businesses and mature-resort attachment. Engagement horizons are hypotheses, not quotas.
