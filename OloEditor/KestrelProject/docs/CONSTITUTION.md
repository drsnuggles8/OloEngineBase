# KESTREL design constitution

Status: consolidated synthesis of owner-approved principles; proposed repository adoption, 2026-11-27. This document governs gameplay intent, not engine architecture or milestone dates.

1. **Fun first; build resorts.** Construction, operation and visible consequences are central. Marketing, trailers and retention metrics do not dictate design. Preserve ambition and use incremental playable validation rather than silently shrinking the vision. (D-001, S-22, V-22, AUD-18)
2. **Easy to understand, difficult to master.** Make interaction intuitive and causal, not shallow. Deep snow, geography, guest behavior and economics create strategic choices; automate routine administration. (D-005, W-04, U-05, AUD-15)
3. **Real geography and recognizable historical progression.** Prefer real data with documented uncertainty/rights; retain fictional maps and gameplay adjustments. Technology opens capabilities; no arbitrary experience-level gates. (D-005, P-01, P-07, AUD-09, AUD-20)
4. **Fair competition and transparent consequences.** AI follows the same authoritative rules, costs and information; difficulty changes starting circumstances, personality and skill, not hidden advantages. No sabotage or arbitrary difficulty escalation. (D-007, A-09, AUD-10)
5. **One coherent authoritative simulation.** Camera, rendering tier, FPS and time speed do not change gameplay. Preserve deterministic state and replay; deliberate inactive-region rules are distinct from rendering optimization. (G-17, AUD-02, AUD-03, AUD-19)
6. **Meaningful freedom and consequence.** One exceptional resort is valid; expansion optional. Creative play, selective customization and modding are supported. No destructive natural disasters, Ironman or intrusive anti-cheat; official completion rules are clear. No artificial retention mechanics. (A-18a, P-13, P-18–P-23, W-16, AUD-03, AUD-12)
7. **Respect attention and creations.** Minimal HUD, optional tutorials, configurable advisors, competent automation, intelligible undo and no surprise hostile takeovers. Never make repetitive clicking the reward. (U-04, U-10–U-12, AUD-01, AUD-11, AUD-15)
8. **Living mountains.** Romantic realism, believable animation, audio, weather, seasonal and historical identity support the experience; graphics scaling preserves decision information and simulation. (U-01, U-13, U-15, U-17, U-18)
9. **Data-driven and reproducible.** Version content, stable IDs, schema validation, migration and provenance; share definitions across gameplay and UI. Preserve authoritative financial and physical invariants. (D-006, AUD-05–AUD-07, AUD-14, AUD-17)
10. **Evidence and owner authority.** Technical correctness and human enjoyment are distinct. Prioritize Steam-first external testing, private consent-based feedback and headless experiments. Agents may propose revisions, not silently change approved design. (D-008, V-01–V-24, AUD-16, AUD-19)

The latest specific dispositions in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) control ambiguities. The engineering north star controls the existing vertical slice. The constitution is not a license to turn hypotheses into requirements.
