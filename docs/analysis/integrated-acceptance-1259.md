# Integrated AAA acceptance (#1259)

The acceptance gate of the #1225 programme: one generated scene that holds every subject at once,
captured on every backend and path, measured under one protocol. This file is the contract (scene,
cameras, manifests, protocol, gate) and, below it, the results. Decisions taken with the owner on
2026-10-10 are recorded on the issue
([comment](https://github.com/drsnuggles8/OloEngineBase/issues/1259#issuecomment-6094959081)).

## Gate

**p95 frame time at or under 16.667 ms with virtual shadow maps on**, native 1920x1080, every
backend x path cell, on the RTX 4090 named below, Release with symbols. A cell passes only if every
counted run passes. Skin, fur and flora are never disabled or thinned to meet it; a miss stays an
open item with an issue.

## Scene: `Scenes/Benchmark/Acceptance.olo`

Generated by `scripts/perf/generate-acceptance-scene.py` from committed scenes (never hand-edited):

| Subject | Source | What it is in this scene |
|---|---|---|
| Flora traversal | `FoliageMeadowToWoodland.olo` | 256 m terrain, six foliage layers (meadow grass, wildflowers, shrubs, leaf litter, dune grass, woodland pines with impostors), sky, shadowed sun; scene wind on (Meadow's wind block) |
| Moving population | `Benchmark/AnimalPopulation.olo` | 41 foxes (hero, 4 featured, 36 herd) with bound coats, turned onto the traversal heading so the herd stands 8-78 m along the meadow-to-woodland walk; every animal walks a closed-form path and follows the ground |
| Short and long coat | `GroomAnimals.olo` | the bound short-coat and long-coat horses, walking in the meadow beside the traversal start, coats parented to their bodies |
| Digital-human close-up | `GroomAnimals.olo`, `DigitalHuman.olo` | the rigged InfiniteScanHead (LookAround clip, skin profile and measured thickness) with its bound hair, on a plinth in a studio beside the terrain, lit by DigitalHuman's key and side lights with the sun as backlight. Separable eyes and mouth do not exist (#1402) |
| Moving light | `Benchmark/Courtyard.olo` | a shadowed point light ("Moving Lantern") carried over the horse station by `EntityMotion` |
| Interaction | `FoliageInteractionComponent` | on the 16 subjects nearest the cameras (the field has 16 slots) |

The traversal's flora was authored for #1224's test walk, not for a picture: 30 m of relief left most
of the ground steep enough to be rock (which excludes every plant), the tops were snow, the woodland
had 19 pines, and 1.2-2.6 m grass hid a fox entirely. The acceptance scene keeps every layer, mesh
and density but sets a 16 m relief, drops the snow rule, gives the grasses meadow heights
(0.45-1.15 m) and doubles the pine density with a wider moisture band, so the walk runs from open
meadow into a wood. These are content choices for legibility, stated here because they change the
workload; nothing was thinned.

Cameras and the moving light are placed from `scripts/perf/acceptance-ground.json`, an 8 m grid of
the real terrain height exported by `AcceptanceSceneTest.ExportsTheGroundHeightGrid`
(`OLO_ACCEPTANCE_GROUND_EXPORT=1`), because the generator cannot evaluate the procedural terrain.
`AcceptanceSceneTest.EveryManifestCameraClearsTheTerrainAlongItsPath` checks every manifest camera
against the height field itself along its whole trajectory (lowest clearance 2.8 m).

Two engine gaps closed for it: animals now stand on the terrain (`AnimalPathComponent::FollowTerrain`),
and a benchmark capture now walks the animal paths (an edit-mode tick never ran the "AnimalPaths"
system, so every earlier capture showed the herd walking on the spot).

## Measurement protocol

`scripts/perf/acceptance-benchmark.py` enforces it:

* Release with symbols only (`/Z7` in the `dev-cached` tree), with a declared compiler. Never compare across builds.
* Interleaved, repeated, fresh-process runs: cells round-robin with the start cell rotated each round, at least three counted runs per cell.
* Other GPU load is checked before every run and sampled every 30 s during it. A run that overlaps
  another GPU process or a build is kept on disk but not counted, and the process is named.
* Per run, per camera: actual internal and display sizes, the resolved upscaler, VSM selected;
  p50/p95/p99/max and deadline misses for frame time, CPU, fence wait, present wait, recording wall
  and join wait, total GPU (valid, distinct frame ids only), and per-pass GPU distributions.
* Provenance per binary: binary, shader-tree and asset hashes, commit, dirty diff and untracked files.
* Statistics are reported as a range across runs; frames are never resampled. The GL test host times
  `Scene::OnUpdateEditor` and the editor host times the completed-frame interval; they are never pooled.
