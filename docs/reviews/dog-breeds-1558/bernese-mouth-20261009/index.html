Bernese lower lip and chin

The lower muzzle has a shallow rounded contour instead of a long straight shelf. The front underside tucks back beneath the nose, the lips have a softer curve, and the centre rises slightly. Gums follow the same shape change; each tooth moves as a rigid piece.

The eyes, nose, body, leg markings, full coat and previous expression tracks are retained. This page compares the previous face with the new lower-mouth pass in the actual engine.

The first two columns are actual OloEditor captures at the same camera and lighting. The real photos have different poses and lighting. Reference guidance: BMDCA breed standard.

Face reference: Sezare, Psinkeric, public domain. Side reference: Berntiers On Demand, Svenska Massan, CC BY 2.0. Photos are unmodified.
