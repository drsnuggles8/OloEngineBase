# The furred dog showcase

`Scenes/Dog.olo` is the groom system's close-up subject (#1533): an original, scruffy
golden-retriever-type dog on a lawn, with a coat you can put the camera into. Where
[groom-animals.md](groom-animals.md) puts every groom child on three animals at range, this scene is
one animal at a face close-up, which is where a coat's density, colour, self-shadow and motion are
judged. `DogShowcaseEvidenceTest` is its headless twin.

## What is in the scene

| entity | what it is |
|---|---|
| `Dog` | the body: `Models/Dog/Dog.gltf`, generated by `build_dog.py` (see its README), eight materials with skin profiles patched over the bare skins, the `Idle` clip playing |
| `DogCoat` | ~300k strands in 18 regions (undercoat and guard hair), bound to the body and GPU-deformed, simulated guides, the coat shadow baked at rest (`m_BakeAtRest`), dual scattering on, a cooked card level for range |
| `DogEyeL` / `DogEyeR` | the eyeballs (`DogEyeball.gltf`) on the eye bones, shaded by the ocular skin profile |
| `Sun` / `Rim` | a warm late-afternoon key (the only shadow caster, 25 m shadow range) and a cool rim from behind |
| `Sky` | a procedural sky with IBL |
| `Lawn` | a flat 60 m terrain under Poly Haven's `grass_medium_01` tuft (CC0): real blades near the camera, the billboard beyond, kept out of the shadow maps (it still receives) |
| `RuntimeCamera` | the three-quarter close-up Play renders from |

## Opening it

TAA runs by itself (the coat is `StochasticAlpha`; see groom-animals.md). Press **Play** for the
clips and the guide simulation; in edit mode the skeleton previews its clip but the coat's solver
does not step, so the coat is carried with the body without swinging.

The clips are `Idle`, `HeadTilt`, `Sit`, `Walk`, `Pant` and `Rest`. Switch them with
`AnimationStateComponent`'s requested clip (the editor's Animation panel, `olo_entity_set_field`,
or `PlayClip` from Lua).

## Regenerating it

1. **The body**: `build_dog.py` (Blender 5.x, headless); see `Models/Dog/README.md`. It writes the
   mesh, the rig, the clips, the eyeball and `DogCoatColor.png`.
2. **The coat, its binding, the skin profiles and the scene**:
   `OLO_DOG_EXPORT=1 OloEngine-Tests --gtest_filter=DogShowcaseEvidenceTest.ExportsTheLiveScene`,
   run from `OloEditor/`. The coat is grown in the fixture from the body's own bind-pose surface,
   cooked and bound exactly as the evidence draws it, imported through the Sandbox project's asset
   manager, and the scene written with `SceneSerializer`.
3. **The cost record**: `OLO_DOG_COST_EXPORT=1 ... --gtest_filter=DogShowcaseEvidenceTest.CostOfTheThreeFramings`
   rewrites `assets/tests/visual/Dog_Cost.txt`. `OLO_DOG_COST=1` measures without writing it.

## What the headless fixture asserts

All on GL, Release, 1280×720 unless named.

- **Furred from every hero angle**: front three-quarter, profile, face close-up, rear with the
  tail, low hero, full body.
- **The eyes sit in their sockets** and look along the rig's gaze, uniformly scaled.
- **Every layer is load-bearing**: fur, the coat's self-shadow, dual scattering and the scene
  shadow each switched off change the frame by several times the repeat floor.
- **The coat stays on the body through every clip**: no binding refused, nothing held at rest, and
  the coat's share of the frame steady.
- **The resolve settles the coat** (B6): shimmer under half the no-history control, at rest and in
  the walk.
- **The LOD ladder** (B7): coverage and linear energy within 10% from a 0.6 m face close-up to
  15 m.
- **The rest bake shadows the moving coat as the pose bake does**: see
  [groom-coat-rest-bake.md](../agent-rules/groom-coat-rest-bake.md).

## The eyes

Each eye is its own globe behind two lid shells, in a socket under a thin skin that leaves an
opening just wider than the lids. The face's fur shortens toward the eye continuously, the lid's
outer skin is furred like the face, and the dark, moist band is only at the margin. Every one of
those choices removed a visible fault: see
[creature-eye-region.md](../agent-rules/creature-eye-region.md). `OLO_DOG_LOOKDEV=1` renders the
left eye close up (`EyeThreeQuarter`) with the rest of the look-development frames.

## Cost

`Dog_Cost.txt` is the per-pass record (1920×1080, the lawn included, runtime frames). The live
editor's numbers, which are the performance gate, are in the PR that shipped the scene.
