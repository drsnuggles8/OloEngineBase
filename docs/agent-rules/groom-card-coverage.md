# Groom card coverage across the hand-over (#1428)

Read before touching `GroomLodBuilder`'s card widths, the per-role width compensation or `GroomCardFibreScales` in `GroomStrandMesh.cpp`, the coat jitter in `GroomCoat.cpp`, the ribbon depth in `GroomStrand.glsl`, the card-tier bake in `GroomRenderPass`, or `GroomAnimalsAcceptanceEvidenceTest.TheCoatKeepsItsCoverageFromNearToFar`. The ladder's own rules: [groom-representation-lod.md](groom-representation-lod.md).

## Rules

1. **Make a card as wide as its members cover, as the strand shader draws them at the hand-over; never their sum.** Members sharing a clump cell overlap: on the long-coated horse they cover 0.40 of their summed width and a summed card tier drew 2.6x the coat. `GroomCardWidth::Covered` integrates, averaged over directions, the members' bands widened to the pixel at `SourcePixelSize`. [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#the-rules)
2. **Bake a card-tier self-shadow at each group's fibre area.** `GroomCardFibreScales` gives each drawn segment its group's base-over-level fibre ratio; a groom-wide factor baked body cards at 2–3x and the tail at two thirds. Apply it wherever a pose is formed (the GPU rest stream builds its pose in two places; scaling one read 25% lighter). [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#the-rules)
3. **Give a card its members' mean jitter:** amplitude / sqrt(N) per group (`CardTierCoat`) for length, width and shade. Full amplitude made lone dense lumps and darkened the card tier 15% more. [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#the-rules)
4. **Draw a ribbon's depth at its fibre's front surface, one radius nearer than its axis.** Only depth moves; screen position and velocity stay the axis's. Drawn at its axis, the body hid card fringes (9% coverage lost on the long coat). [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#the-rules)
5. **Start a ribbon's coat-shadow march on the tube's lit side, one radius from the axis** (#1533, `oloGroomCoatTauAndBody`). From the axis, cards shadowed themselves (0.74–0.80 of strands vs 1.05–1.06). A backlit card reads too bright. [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#the-rules)
6. **Apply the strand budget's width compensation per role, in the build,** not as one groom-wide `1 / achieved` (it widened unthinned guard hair 2.2x). [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#the-rules)
7. **Measure a hand-over in linear quantities, apart, on a frozen pose, from the coat's open side.** Coverage from the entity-ID target, linear luma from HDR scene colour on those pixels, no temporal resolve, no background subtraction; report coverage, radiance (kappa = 0), shadow, energy separately (an 8-bit |coat − body| contrast hid a 30% loss); use kappa = 0, not the component switched off (that brings back the root ramp); freeze clips; keep the Near stop as the identical-strands control (reads 1); view each coat from its open side. [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#the-rules)
8. **Judge the representation on one converged volume** (128^3, half-voxel steps) before blaming either it or the shadow volume (#1508). [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#the-rules)
9. **Widen a card to what its members cover at the pixel it is drawn at (#1558).** Each card point carries summed / covered (`GroomLodLevel::PointCoverageGrowth`, format v5); the shader fixes `E` from the cooked width and widens along `(E + f)(1 - exp(-S / (E + f)))` (`GroomCardCoverageWiden`). Laws without `E` overshoot (linear share 13% over, `f(1 - exp(-S/f))` 27%); an unwidened card fell 10–15% short at 45 m. [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#the-rules)
10. **Scale growth by reach (#1595):** the stream carries `1 + reach (growth - 1)`, reach `(strands per card - 1) / 31` capped at 1 (`GroomCardCoverageGrowthReach`, in `GroomBuildSource::FromLevel`). Horses (9 and 14 per card) need none; foxes (26–30) and dogs (45–127) fall 4–15% short without it. [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#the-rules)
11. **Size the card cell by strands per card, not by the dog (#1558).** The cell sets how far a card averages its lock: 12 mm left adult dogs 7–9% darker; 9 mm met the 5% bar; full-fur breeds (#1608, #1621: over 100 per card at 9 mm) need 6 mm (60–70 per card). `tools/dog-authoring/set_card_tier.py` re-authors a committed archive's cell. Widening fibre lobes by tangent spread does not help. [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#the-rules)
12. **Hold the dog's hand-over test.** `DogShowcaseEvidenceTest.TheCardHandOverStepsTheCoatWithinTheNearLaddersBar` holds 17–27 m and keeps cards within the near ladder's 5% step bar. `m_CardPixelSize` 0.5 is the smallest threshold the policy keeps; 0 reads as unset and falls back to 256. [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#the-dogs-hand-over-held-1533)
13. **Do not pursue these:** the exact geometric union (`SourcePixelSize = 0`) or a cook at the real hand-over size (1–2%); a per-point fibre scale (within 2–3% of per group, needs a format change); `MaxWidthCompensation` in pass stats (a maximum over every groom drawn). [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#what-does-not-help)
14. **Expect shipped-shadow-LOD energy up to 1.4x for cards.** The hand-over bakes strands at 16^3 and cards at 8^3; the contract holds within 10% on a converged volume, the shipped cells hold a gross guard (0.75–1.40), filed as #1508. [Details](groom-card-coverage-details.md#measured)
