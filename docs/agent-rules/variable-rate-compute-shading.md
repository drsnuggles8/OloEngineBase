# Variable Rate Compute Shading: the saving is the easy half

Issue #683. Read before touching `VRCSClassify.comp`, `include/VRCS.glsl`, `ShadingRateClassifier`, or before adopting VRCS in a second compute pass. A classifier rates each 8x8 screen tile with a footprint of 1, 2 or 4; per invocation, leaders compute and broadcast, followers retire. Every defect is quiet: nothing crashes and no test goes red on its own.

## Rules

1. **Never use `barrier()` for the VRCS broadcast; keep `include/VRCS.glsl` communication-free.** Consuming kernels have early returns (GTAO has four), and a `barrier()` that only some invocations reach is undefined behaviour. Each invocation reads one rate texel, computes `OloVRCSIsLeader` from its own coordinates, and retires or writes its own block. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#2-barrier-is-why-the-broadcast-is-per-invocation-and-not-shared-memory)
2. **In `VRCSClassify.comp` no invocation may return above the reduction.** Out-of-viewport invocations contribute identities (`+FLT_MAX` for min, `-FLT_MAX` for max, zero counts) and stay alive through all barrier steps. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#2-barrier-is-why-the-broadcast-is-per-invocation-and-not-shared-memory)
3. **Grant coarsening; never assume it.** Every threshold is an upper bound a tile must clear; an unclassified tile, a sky/geometry mix, a sentinel normal, an unwritten rate texel or a rate outside `{1,2,4}` resolves to full rate. `OloVRCSDecodeFootprint` rejects unknown values, and `GTAORenderPass` keys the consume flag on `m_VRCSActiveLastExecute`, not on settings. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#3-coarsening-must-be-granted-never-assumed)
4. **Keep all three signals: depth planarity, normal agreement, previous-frame luminance.** Normal agreement (`1 - |mean(n)|`) catches creases (`VRCSClassifierGpuTest.ANormalCreaseAtConstantDepthKeepsThatTileFullRate`); luminance catches shadow edges and highlights, is optional, and its absence makes classification stricter. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#3-coarsening-must-be-granted-never-assumed)
5. **Measure depth departure from a plane, not depth range.** Use the maximum residual of a least-squares plane fit over inverse depth, relative to the tile's mean inverse depth; a depth range test punishes slope and rejected most of an outdoor frame. The fit needs three sums, one extra reduction round, and a complete tile, so a partial tile at the screen edge never coarsens. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#3a-measure-departure-from-a-plane-not-depth-range--and-only-the-heatmap-could-tell)
6. **Keep a positive test that something coarsens.** `ASteeplyRecedingPlaneStillCoarsens` asserts "this must coarsen"; a suite of only "must not coarsen" assertions passes for an inert feature. Look at the rate heatmap before trusting scalar image diffs. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#3a-measure-departure-from-a-plane-not-depth-range--and-only-the-heatmap-could-tell)
7. **Reject a sky/geometry mix on the mix itself, not on the depth range.** The far plane linearises to a finite distance, so a horizon tile's range is large only by luck. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#3a-measure-departure-from-a-plane-not-depth-range--and-only-the-heatmap-could-tell)
8. **Preserve the partition property.** Footprints align to the pixel-space origin (1, 2, 4 divide the 8-pixel tile), leaders sit at the block's minimum corner, and leader blocks tile the plane exactly once. A consumer bounds-checks only the broadcast, never the dispatch. `VRCSContractTest` counts writes per pixel across a 2x2 tile block and requires exactly one. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#4-the-partition-property-and-the-two-ways-to-break-it)
9. **Never use a footprint that does not divide the tile.** A leader's block would straddle two tiles with different rates. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#4-the-partition-property-and-the-two-ways-to-break-it)
10. **Retire whole waves, do not mask lanes.** A wave costs what its slowest active lane costs; masking 24 of 32 lanes saved nothing (GTAO got 43% slower). `GTAO.comp`'s `ResolveVRCSWorkItem` hands the 256 invocations the concatenated leader lists of the four 8x8 tiles, so contiguous spare lanes fall off the work list. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#4a-masking-lanes-is-not-retiring-waves-and-only-the-second-one-is-faster)
11. **Keep the disabled path on the identity mapping.** `ResolveVRCSWorkItem` returns `gl_GlobalInvocationID` when VRCS is off, so the feature does not perturb the access pattern it is disabled on. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#4a-masking-lanes-is-not-retiring-waves-and-only-the-second-one-is-faster)
12. **Broadcast a per-pixel output only after asking what reads it, and check it against what the classifier guarantees.** GTAO's edge weight is broadcast too: inside a certified coarse tile there is no discontinuity to protect, and keeping any per-pixel output stops every wave from retiring. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#5-per-pixel-data-must-stay-per-pixel--until-it-stops-the-waves-retiring)
13. **Read the mirrored constants from the shader text in tests.** `OLO_VRCS_TILE_SIZE` and the three rate encodings exist in both `include/VRCS.glsl` and `ShadingRateClassifier`; the classify shader's `local_size` must equal the tile size because `Classify()` dispatches the tile grid itself. `VRCSContractTest` reads the shader text instead of restating constants. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#6-two-mirrored-numbers-and-the-slot-the-feature-deliberately-did-not-take)
14. **Do not spend a new UBO binding or `TEX_*` slot.** Classifier params ride `UBO_USER_0` (binding 7); the rate texture rides a pass-local heap-offset index (6, next to GTAO's 3/4/5), because a new engine texture slot moves `MAX_ENGINE_TEXTURE_SLOTS` and `HEAP_IMAGE_SLOT_BASE`, mirrored by hand in `BindlessHeap.glsl`. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#6-two-mirrored-numbers-and-the-slot-the-feature-deliberately-did-not-take)
15. **Write the rate heatmap into the AO term ahead of every early-out, and suppress denoise in Execute only.** `Setup` still declares the ping/pong scratch so toggling the overlay does not change graph topology. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#6-two-mirrored-numbers-and-the-slot-the-feature-deliberately-did-not-take)
16. **Bind both slots unconditionally with a type-matching stand-in.** The classifier's previous-colour slot falls back to scene depth; GTAO's rate slot falls back to the Hilbert LUT, the only other unsigned sampler it binds, since a float stand-in read through `usampler2D` returns garbage. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#6-two-mirrored-numbers-and-the-slot-the-feature-deliberately-did-not-take)
17. **When adopting VRCS in a second pass, follow the seven steps.** Include `../include/VRCS.glsl` and branch on `OloVRCSIsLeader`; route every store of the coarsenable output through a bounds-tested footprint loop; leave per-pixel outputs per-pixel; bind the rate texture with `ShadingRateClassifier::GetRateLifetime()` (`Persistent`); gate on `Classify()`'s return value; replace the frame stamp (today `GTAORenderPass`'s Execute counter) with a renderer-wide monotonic frame index, since `InflightFrameManager::GetCurrentFrameIndex` is a ring position; hash the VRCS gate into the blackboard fingerprint if `Setup()` branches on it. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#7-adopting-vrcs-in-a-second-pass)
18. **Check the shape of every pass an issue names before ordering work on it.** Only GTAO takes screen-tile VRCS as designed; `DeferredLightingPass` and `SSGIRenderPass` are fragment draws (port to compute first); `VolumetricFogPass` dispatches over a 160x90x64 froxel volume, so classify in froxel XY and measure before building. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#1-the-issues-premise-was-wrong-about-three-of-its-four-passes)
19. **Measure the whole bracket, with a control.** Report classify + consumer + the untouched denoise dispatch (`VRCSPerfProbe`, DISABLED_, an instrument not a gate); the control must not move, components must reconcile, and the result must reproduce. Quote the coarse-tile fraction with any number. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#8-measuring-it-honestly)
20. **Judge the visual claim by a tile-boundary step ratio, not a mean image diff.** `VRCSVisualEvidenceTest` measures mean step across 8-pixel tile boundaries against within-tile on smooth ground, as a ratio against the full-rate frame. [Details](variable-rate-compute-shading-details.md#8-measuring-it-honestly)
