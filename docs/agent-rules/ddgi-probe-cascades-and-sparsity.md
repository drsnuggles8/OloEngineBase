# DDGI probe cascades, toroidal storage and request-driven sparsity

Issue #707 turned the single authored DDGI volume of #632 into a camera-centred clipmap with request-driven updates and GPU relocation. Every failure here is silent and plausible: the frame renders and no test goes red. Read before touching `Renderer/DDGI/*`, `include/DDGICommon.glsl`, `include/DDGIProbeBuffers.glsl` or any `compute/DDGI_*.comp`.

## Rules

1. **Keep cascade storage toroidal: storage coordinate `s` holds the lattice point congruent to `s` modulo `Dims` inside the window.** A one-cell camera shift then reassigns exactly one slab; otherwise every probe invalidates on every step (capture is six draws per probe, ADR 0007). `DDGIMath.ToroidalStorageInvalidatesOnlyOneSlabPerCellOfCameraMotion` pins the count; check it is not passing because nothing changed. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#1-toroidal-storage-is-the-load-bearing-invariant-and-breaking-it-looks-like-smeared-gi)
2. **Invalidate the changed slab, deriving it independently on both sides from the same two lattice origins.** CPU: `DDGIProbeUpdatePass::BuildCascades`; GPU: `compute/DDGI_ProbeMaintain.comp`. Do not let the GPU inform the CPU (that is a readback). A missed slab shows as GI smeared behind the camera, easily misread as hysteresis. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#1-toroidal-storage-is-the-load-bearing-invariant-and-breaking-it-looks-like-smeared-gi)
3. **Use Euclidean modulo (`DDGI::WrapIndex` / `ddgiWrapIndex`), never `%`.** `%` truncates toward zero in C++ and GLSL, and negative lattice coordinates (camera toward -x) then index another tile. `DDGIMath.NegativeLatticeCoordinatesWrapIntoTheStorageWindow` asserts `LatticeMin.x < 0` first. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#2--truncates-toward-zero-in-both-c-and-glsl)
4. **Keep the camera seed floor in `DDGI_ProbeMaintain.comp`.** It requests every probe within `DDGICameraSeedRadius` unconditionally; without it one bad frame of the screen request chain lets GI fade out silently. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#3-sparsitys-failure-mode-is-no-gi-and-no-error)
5. **Keep the probe-to-probe request hop (`DDGI_RequestProbe.comp`); keep it exactly one indirection deep.** Dropping it makes bounce GI too dark; unbounded transitive requests converge on the dense grid. `DDGIProbeAuxRecord::ScreenRequestFrame` enforces it (the hop walks only screen-marked probes; its requests are non-screen). Measure `GetProbeStats()`. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#3-sparsitys-failure-mode-is-no-gi-and-no-error)
6. **Gate relight and blend with the same predicate `ddgiProbeUpdatesNow`** (used by both `DDGI_Relight.glsl` and `DDGI_BlendIrradiance.glsl`). A probe that blends without relighting drifts toward stale lighting. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#4-the-relight-and-blend-gates-must-be-the-same-predicate)
7. **For a non-updating probe in the ping-ponged irradiance atlas, copy `prev` through; never `discard`.** Discarding leaves two-frames-old content that flickers at half the update rate. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#4-the-relight-and-blend-gates-must-be-the-same-predicate)
8. **Add no readback in `Execute` (issue #707 acceptance criterion 3).** Relocation and classification live in `compute/DDGI_Relocate.comp`; `DDGIProbeUpdatePass::ProbeRecord` splits CPU-owned fields (`CaptureCount`, `LastCaptureFrame`) from GPU-owned mirrors (`OffsetN`, `State`, `Bounce*`) that read as defaults until `ReadbackProbeDiagnostics()` is called. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#5-there-is-no-readback-in-execute-and-that-is-a-contract)
9. **Derive the capture position on the GPU.** `DDGI_Capture.glsl`'s vertex stage subtracts the relocated position; the CPU supplies an eye-at-origin view-projection and the probe's global index. Capturing from the lattice point while relight reconstructs from the relocated one offsets cached hits by up to 0.45 cell. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#5-there-is-no-readback-in-execute-and-that-is-a-contract)
10. **Make the CPU liveness proxy `IsProbeCpuLive` a superset of what the GPU can request.** Use a frustum test dilated by the hop's reach (one max ray distance to the hit point plus one cell); the undilated version left 30 of 509 live probes uncaptured forever. Check containment, not similarity; a non-zero fixed point of `UncapturedLive` is the signal and `CascadeFieldIsTemporallyStableFromAFixedPose` asserts it (`GetCapturedFraction()` cannot see it). [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#5-there-is-no-readback-in-execute-and-that-is-a-contract)
11. **Do not let `ReadbackProbeDiagnostics()` mutate scheduling state.** It deliberately does not copy the GPU captured flag into `ProbeRecord::CaptureCount`. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#5-there-is-no-readback-in-execute-and-that-is-a-contract)
12. **Treat `DDGI::RelocateProbeSpring`'s step size as a stability parameter.** `kSpringStepScale = 0.35` settles in 3-4 captures; near 1 oscillates between cells. The ellipsoid clamp projects rather than rejects, and the sky counts as a free direction with full weight. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#6-the-spring-relocation-is-a-feedback-loop-so-its-step-size-is-a-stability-parameter)
13. **End the warm-up on a capture that runs classification but not the spring.** `DDGI::kRelocationWarmupCaptures` (`kMaxRelocationIterations` spring applications plus one settling capture), derived with the tier and spring suppression from one function, `DDGI::RelocationStepForCapture` (#1279). Dropping the settling capture leaves 0.10 cells of displaced hit cache in the `DDGIMathTest` closed-loop model. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#the-warm-up-must-end-on-a-capture-that-does-not-move-the-probe)
14. **Quote hit-cache resolution together with a footprint figure when changing cascade defaults.** Shipped 4 x 16^3: ~68 MB at t = 8 (cascade path, ~4.3 KB/probe), ~128 MB at t = 16 (authored volume, ~8.0 KB/probe); PGI's 6 x 32^3 is ~0.8 GB / ~1.5 GB. Breakdown is in `RenderingPath.h`; the renderer-settings panel prints probe count and footprint. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#7-cascade-defaults-are-a-memory-decision-not-a-quality-one)
15. **Verify in order:** `DDGIMathTest` (L1); `DDGIReferenceParityTest` (probe irradiance vs `PathTracer::EstimateIrradiance`; the authored path must stay bit-identical, so a parity move there means the compatibility claim broke); multi-angle screenshots from `SandboxProject/Assets/Scenes/DDGICascadesTest.olo` (120 m corridor, no authored volume); convergence over many frames; measured `GetProbeStats()` counts. [Details](ddgi-probe-cascades-and-sparsity-details.md#8-what-to-verify-and-in-what-order)
