# Audio voice budget — stealing and virtualization (issue #730)

`Audio/VoiceManager.{h,cpp}` caps concurrently audible voices, ranks contenders, and keeps losers running silently so they can return mid-stream. Read before touching anything that starts, stops, or scores a sound.

## Rules

1. **Put the budget inside `AudioSource::Play()` and `SoundGraphSound::Play()`, not at call sites.** Sounds start from at least six places (`Scene::InitAudioRuntime`, `Scene::InitializeAudioSoundGraph`, `SceneStreamer`, `AudioEventsManager::Update`, `ScriptGlue.cpp`, `LuaScriptGlue.cpp`). `Play()` means "registered, audible when worth a slot", never a guarantee of output. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#1-there-is-no-such-thing-as-the-playback-call-site--put-the-budget-inside-play)
2. **Keep `VoiceManager` free of the backend.** It is a pure decision unit over `VoiceParams` driven through `IVoiceHost`, with no miniaudio or `ma_sound`, because a real `AudioSource` cannot be built headless. `OloEngine/tests/Audio/VoiceManagerTest.cpp` tests every rule with a recording `FakeVoiceHost`. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#2-the-policy-object-must-not-know-about-the-backend)
3. **Assert the resume position, not `IsPlaying()`.** A restored looping voice must resume at the position it would have reached (`position += dt * pitch`, wrapped modulo clip length); the manager advances it while virtual. Assert `LastStartPosition`. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#3-its-playing-again-is-not-the-assertion--the-position-is)
4. **Keep `CanBecomeAudible` clause order: paused, was ever audible, looping, unknown length (`DurationSeconds == 0`), otherwise only from position 0.** The test is whether starting now drops the player into a sound they never heard start. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#3-its-playing-again-is-not-the-assertion--the-position-is)
5. **Treat the backend cursor as authoritative while a voice is audible (`OnVoiceQueryPosition`).** Integrating `dt` drifts against the device clock. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#3-its-playing-again-is-not-the-assertion--the-position-is)
6. **Keep `kDefaultPromotionMargin` (0.02) hysteresis.** A virtual voice takes a slot only by beating the audible one by more than the margin; without it two near-equal voices swap every tick. The margin also guarantees the steal loop terminates; the iteration cap in `Rebalance` exists for a zero margin. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#4-hysteresis-is-a-correctness-requirement-not-a-polish-item)
7. **Never call a host with the data lock held, and always lock transition-then-data.** `OnVoiceStart`/`OnVoiceStop` are queued as `PendingTransition`s under `m_Mutex` and applied after release; `m_TransitionMutex` spans decide-then-apply. No `IVoiceHost` callback may re-enter `Acquire`/`Release`/`Update`/`SetMaxVoices`; `OnVoiceQueryPosition` runs under the data lock and must not re-enter either. `Scene::UpdateAudio` is `.Parallelizable()`. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#5-never-call-a-host-with-the-lock-held)
8. **Owner pause outranks the budget.** `VoiceParams::Paused` scores the voice zero and makes `CanBecomeAudible` false, so the budget may take a paused slot but never gives it back; only `SetVoicePaused(handle, false)` revives it. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#6-owner-intent-outranks-budget-policy--the-pause-trap)
9. **Restart the backend in `AudioSource::UnPause` after clearing the pause if the budget still reports it audible.** An uncontended pause keeps its slot and emits no transition, so nothing calls `OnVoiceStart`; without this the source is "playing" but mute. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#6-owner-intent-outranks-budget-policy--the-pause-trap)
10. **`Paused` is manager-owned.** `UpdateParams` ignores the incoming value and carries its own forward, because `Scene::UpdateAudio` pushes params for every source every frame. `WasAudible` keeps a heard, paused, unpaused one-shot from being refused. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#6-owner-intent-outranks-budget-policy--the-pause-trap)
11. **Retire voices and release them in `Stop()` and the destructor.** The manager auto-retires non-looping voices past `DurationSeconds`; `DurationSeconds == 0` voices are never auto-retired and the owner must `Release` them in `Stop()` and in the destructor (the manager holds a raw `IVoiceHost*`). [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#7-a-slot-is-leaked-unless-something-retires-the-voice)
12. **Virtualize clips by `ma_sound_stop`, graphs by freeze.** Clip: stop; devirtualize with `ma_sound_seek_to_pcm_frame(position)` + start. Graph: mute `Volume` and `SetVoiceSuspended(true)`; thaw then un-mute. Suspending skips the whole graph step, so it needs no per-node save/restore (#745). [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#8-the-two-backends-virtualize-differently-and-the-difference-is-not-a-gap-in-the-api)
13. **Do not stop the graph and re-raise `SendPlayEvent()` on devirtualization, and do not reuse `SoundGraphSource::SuspendProcessing` for the budget.** That restarts every node; `SuspendProcessing` is the graph-swap handshake and its resume zeroes the playback counters. Keep the freeze a separate flag. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#8-the-two-backends-virtualize-differently-and-the-difference-is-not-a-gap-in-the-api)
14. **Accept that a graph voice cannot resume at the position it would have reached.** For a procedural graph, advancing to frame N is the DSP work of frames 0..N; a catch-up burst in one audio callback underruns. `VoiceManager::Update` re-syncs the record to the graph's real frame via `OnVoiceQueryPosition`. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#8-the-two-backends-virtualize-differently-and-the-difference-is-not-a-gap-in-the-api)
15. **Keep a graph `Play()` virtualized across the whole admission decision.** Release the old handle with `resumePlayback=false` and let `OnVoiceStart` (driven synchronously by `Acquire` for a winner) be the only thing that thaws. A voice starting over budget is never handed to `OnVoiceStop`; un-muting first leaves a window of previous-stream audio. `AudioSource` starts `ma_sound` only from `OnVoiceStart`. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#8-the-two-backends-virtualize-differently-and-the-difference-is-not-a-gap-in-the-api)
16. **Leave a stopped or completed graph voice frozen, and lift the other suspension axis in `Play()` only.** `ReleaseVoice(resumePlayback=false)` leaves it muted and suspended. `SoundGraphSource::Update` parks a finished source with `SuspendProcessing(true)`; `Play` clears it (counter reset is correct there since `SendPlayEvent` restarts the stream). Never move that clear onto `OnVoiceStart`, which would turn every resume into a restart. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#8-the-two-backends-virtualize-differently-and-the-difference-is-not-a-gap-in-the-api)
17. **Invert priority in `BuildVoiceParams`.** `SoundGraphSound::m_Priority` is miniaudio-flavoured (0 = highest); `VoiceParams::Priority` is 1 = highest. A reversed polarity steals the most important sounds first and still passes voice-count tests. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#8-the-two-backends-virtualize-differently-and-the-difference-is-not-a-gap-in-the-api)
18. **Adding a field to `AudioSourceConfig` is four edits.** (1) the field in `Audio/AudioSource.h`; (2) both serialize and deserialize blocks in `Scene/SceneSerializer.cpp`, with the `TrySetDsp` clamp (a missing key keeps the default); (3) the `Serialize()` overload in `SaveGame/SaveGameComponentSerializer.cpp`, silent if forgotten, and bump `kSaveGameFormatVersion`; (4) the `OLO_PROPERTY` annotation in `Scene/Components.h`. `AudioSourceComponent` is hand-serialized (fields behind a private `m_Cold` blob). [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#9-adding-a-field-to-audiosourceconfig-is-four-edits-and-one-of-them-is-silent)
19. **Put new authored audio state in `AudioSourceColdData` / `AudioSourceConfig`.** `static_assert(sizeof(AudioSourceComponent) <= 32)` forbids inline fields. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#9-adding-a-field-to-audiosourceconfig-is-four-edits-and-one-of-them-is-silent)
20. **Re-score on every input that moves.** Score is `Priority x gain x distanceAttenuation`, so `SetVolume`, `SetPitch`, `SetLooping`, `SetPosition`, `SetMinDistance` / `SetMaxDistance`, `SetSpatialization`, `SetPriority` and `SetConfig` must each push fresh params. Tick the manager last in `Scene::UpdateAudio`, after listener sync, per-source position sync and `AudioEventsManager::Update`. [Details](audio-voice-budget-details.md#10-re-score-on-every-input-that-moves)
