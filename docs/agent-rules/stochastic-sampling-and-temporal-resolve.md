# Stochastic sampling and temporal resolve

Rules for the shared blue-noise sampler and temporal resolve kernel, and any pass that adopts them.
Applies to: `OloEditor/assets/shaders/include/StochasticCommon.glsl`, `include/TemporalResolve.glsl`,
the VNDF block in `include/PBRCommon.glsl`, `OloEngine/src/OloEngine/Renderer/BlueNoise.h`.
Every defect here renders a plausible image, so each rule needs its own test.

## Rules

1. **Always pair the VNDF weight with its negative control.** Multiply VNDF-sampled specular by `ggxVNDFWeight()` (`G2/G1`); omitting it is one-signed bias (+1% to +7% at roughness 0.4, +17% to +19% at 0.7). Test against brute-force integration (`StochasticSampler.VndfEstimatorMatchesBruteForce`) and assert the unweighted form fails it. Use `alpha = roughness²`, the matching `ggxSmithLambda`, and Schlick on `dot(V, H)`. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#4-the-vndf-weight-is-the-silent-failure-archetype)
2. **Verify call-graph reach (one grep) before pricing a correctness fix as moving every golden.** [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#4-the-vndf-weight-is-the-silent-failure-archetype)
3. **Rotate only toroidal sample dimensions; stratify and jitter the rest.** An azimuth may take a Cranley-Patterson rotation; a radius, distance or mip must use `u1 = (rayIndex + blue.x) / rayCount`. Pinned by `StochasticSampler.DimensionZeroStaysStratifiedAcrossSamples` with a paired negative. A CPU model of one sample on the unit square flatters the wrong form, so render the real pass. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#3a-a-blue-noise-rotation-is-only-valid-on-a-dimension-that-wraps)
4. **Assert the spectrum of the error image, not the sample values.** Blue noise moves error to high frequencies; uniform values and the right mean are true of white noise too. Pair any metric both formulations could pass with an assertion the replaced one fails. Quote the roughly 4x gain, not the raw tile's 5000x. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#1-blue-noise-is-a-claim-about-error-not-about-numbers)
5. **When comparing samplers on a rendered frame, measure spectral peakiness and look at the amplified residual, not only RMS.** Structured lattice noise is worse than unstructured noise of equal energy. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#3b-structured-noise-is-the-artifact-rms-will-not-show-it)
6. **Generate the tile with void-and-cluster at startup; keep the kernel wrapping.** A non-wrapping kernel leaves a faint grid at the tile period. Do not enlarge past 64x64 (cost O(pixels²): 12 ms vs 466 ms, no spectral gain). [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#2-you-cannot-evaluate-blue-noise-procedurally)
7. **Generate the two channels independently, and mix a stream seed into every draw.** Never derive channel 2 from channel 1 (`fract(first * golden)`; see [glsl-shaders.md](glsl-shaders.md) §11), and never use `seed * <generator increment>` as a start offset. Pinned by `StochasticSampler.TileChannelsAreIndependent` (cross-channel correlation), since every per-channel metric passed on the bug. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#2-you-cannot-evaluate-blue-noise-procedurally)
8. **Assert both halves of tile animation.** Consecutive frames must be redrawn (cross-frame correlation), and each advanced frame must still be blue; the value shift `fract(tile + frameIndex * R2)` costs some of the property. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#3-animating-the-tile-costs-some-of-the-property-and-it-is-not-obvious)
9. **Advance `StochasticFrameIndex` unconditionally; freeze what a pass samples with until something averages it.** It advances once per frame in `RenderPipeline`, wrapped to 2^20 (exact in `f32`). A pass with no accumulator samples with a frozen index. Do not reuse `TAAJitterFrameIndex` or `CloudFrameIndex`. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#3-animating-the-tile-costs-some-of-the-property-and-it-is-not-obvious)
10. **Clip the history toward the neighbourhood centre; do not clamp componentwise.** Componentwise clamping shifts hue. Test disocclusion on relative view-space distance, never device depth. Give motion-scaled feedback a sub-pixel dead zone. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#5-a-temporal-resolve-clips-it-does-not-clamp)
11. **Keep a non-colour alpha out of YCoCg.** Give alpha its own 1-D box in the same nine-tap loop (`OloTemporalClipHistoryScalar`, which is the clip degenerate; `StochasticSampler.ScalarClipIsTheOneDimensionalCaseOfTheClip`). Do not drop the hard min/max next to the variance box (fp32 sqrt keeps a flat box about ±4e-4 wide). Assert on the resolved value, not `stddev == 0`. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#5a-when-the-signal-is-rgba-and-alpha-is-not-a-colour)
12. **GPU-probe the gather, not the math.** Use `ShaderUnit_TemporalResolveRGBA.glsl` with flat-colour/varied-alpha neighbourhoods and the mirror to catch crossed accumulators. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#5a-when-the-signal-is-rgba-and-alpha-is-not-a-colour)
13. **Say in a comment which parts of the shared kernel a pass declines and why.** The cloudscape deliberately does not take `OloTemporalMotionFeedback`. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#5a-when-the-signal-is-rgba-and-alpha-is-not-a-colour)
14. **Adopt the sampler in order.** `#define OLO_BLUE_NOISE_GLOBAL_SAMPLER` after `include/BindlessHeap.glsl`, then `#include "include/StochasticCommon.glsl"`; in C++ call `CreateBlueNoiseTexture()` / `DestroyBlueNoiseTexture()` / `BindBlueNoiseTexture()` from `Init()`, the destructor and `Execute()`. Each pass owns its own texture; share only the CPU tile `BlueNoise::GetTileRG()` (see [lazy-static-release-ownership.md](lazy-static-release-ownership.md)). [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#6-adopting-the-utility-in-a-new-pass)
15. **Never sample the tile in a shader that also declares uniform or storage block 17.** `TEX_BLUE_NOISE` is slot 17, shared with `UBO_FOG` and `SSBO_INSTANCE_DRAW_INDIRECT`; `StochasticSampler.NoShaderCollidesWithTheBlueNoiseSlot` checks it. A slot above `TEX_SHADER_GRAPH_0` is also a shader edit (README §2). [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#6-adopting-the-utility-in-a-new-pass)
16. **Build a signal-only history before adding a resolve.** A pass compositing into scene colour is not accumulable. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#6-adopting-the-utility-in-a-new-pass)
17. **Structure a retrofitted resolve as three draws in one render-graph node.** Signal, resolve, composite, with the two intermediates as graph scratch under the pass's gate and `AllowSamePassReadWrite`; copy `CloudscapeRenderPass`. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#6a-retrofitting-a-resolve-onto-a-compositing-pass-four-things-that-are-not-obvious)
18. **Accumulate a signal whose miss value is zero.** SSGI accumulates raw indirect diffuse; SSR accumulates `(reflection - base) * blend` and composites `base + delta`. Test that a miss contributes exactly 0. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#6a-retrofitting-a-resolve-onto-a-compositing-pass-four-things-that-are-not-obvious)
19. **Pack positive view depth into the signal alpha, and write alpha on every early-out.** Sky and roughness-rejected pixels that skip it read as permanent disocclusion. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#6a-retrofitting-a-resolve-onto-a-compositing-pass-four-things-that-are-not-obvious)
20. **Fill the has-history UBO lane from `Execute`, not at configure time.** Patch it with a partial `SetData(&vec4, sizeof(vec4), offsetof(...))` (or `SetHistory()` after `PopulateBlackboard`). Hash a new history flag into the blackboard fingerprint ([render-pipeline-caches.md](render-pipeline-caches.md)); use `OLO_RG_POISON_TRANSIENTS` / `OLO_RG_DISABLE_ALIASING` ([render-graph-transient-aliasing.md](render-graph-transient-aliasing.md)) if accumulation reads another buffer. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#6a-retrofitting-a-resolve-onto-a-compositing-pass-four-things-that-are-not-obvious)
21. **Keep the GLSL Sobol' bit-exact with `Renderer/PathTracing/PathSampler.h`, with a pixel-independent seed.** Pinned by `GlslSobolMirrorsPathSampler`, `ShaderCarriesThePathSamplerConstants` and `AllPixelsShareOneSequenceOffsetByTheTile`; a per-pixel seed makes errors white. [Details](stochastic-sampling-and-temporal-resolve-details.md#7-two-samplers-in-one-engine)

## Commands and checks

A/B two shader versions with no rebuild (swap the `.glsl`, rerun, diff the PNGs in `OloEditor/assets/tests/visual/`):

```
OloEngine-Tests.exe --gtest_filter='SSGIVisualEvidence*'
```

Validate the instrument first: patch the pass to emit `indirectDiffuse = vec3(0, 1, 0)` and confirm the output moves. `olo_screenshot` and `olo_render_capture_target` do not reflect live post-process settings; pre-#906 checkouts must delete `OloEditor/assets/cache/shader/opengl/<Name>.glsl.cached_*` between arms.
