# Groom representation LOD: the ladder, the budgets and the compensation (#1252)

Read before touching `OloEngine/src/OloEngine/Groom/GroomLod*.{h,cpp}`, `GroomStrandMesh.{h,cpp}`'s `GroomBuildSource`, `Scene::PublishGroomStrandRequests`'s LOD block, `GroomRoleWidthCompensation` in `GroomStrandMesh.cpp`, or section 10 of the `.ologroom` format.

## Rules

1. **Compensate coverage by `1/k`, linear; never `1/sqrt(k)`.** A strand is a band, so a coat's area goes as count x width. `FoliageLod::CoverageCompensation` uses `1/sqrt(k)` for sprites and under-compensates a sixteenth-density coat by 4x. Compensated arms land in 0.98–1.06. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
2. **Compensate on the achieved fraction, per role, not the requested one.** The budget is an integer stride per role (`GroomStrandMesh.cpp`); each role is widened by the inverse of what its own stride kept (`GroomRoleWidthCompensation`, #1428). See [groom-card-coverage.md](groom-card-coverage.md). [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
3. **Move budgets in halvings, with the compensation in the cached stream.** The strand geometry is cached on build settings; a sliding budget rebuilds every vertex buffer every frame. The width compensation is built per role into that stream with the cap in the cache key, so a stride step and its compensation change in one rebuild. A halving cross-fades like a hand-over ([groom-lod-cross-fade-and-detail-bias.md](groom-lod-cross-fade-and-detail-bias.md) rule 13). [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
4. **Make a card a kept strand widened to what its cluster covers; not an average, not the sum.** The summed card drew 2.6x a dense coat (#1428); the mean centreline lost 45% of the short coat's area (0.548 vs 0.963). `GroomCardAggregation::MeanCentreline` survives only as the rejected alternative. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
5. **Cook a card level only where it is above the compensation cap.** Runtime widening is capped at 8x; at a 59x reduction the matched strand arm carries 0.23 of the area and the card 1.01. A cell reducing only 4–9x is not worth cooking; the builder's "no reduction" refusal is a floor. The silhouette claim is two assertions: never materially worse (5% tolerance) in the card band, and a clear win at and below 64 px. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
6. **Send every tier through one shader.** The card is a cooked curve, so the fibre BCSDF (#1247), coat shadow volume (#1248), per-strand tint (#1251) and guide simulation (#1250) keep working at card range. Do not bake a card texture. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
7. **Give every LOD level a source map back to the base groom.** The binding rest frame and the guide influence table are indexed by base curve; without the map the coat detaches and stops moving at card range. `Scene` remaps selected curves before root transforms; `GroomCooker::Canonicalize` remaps it on reorder. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
8. **Keep root UVs inside ±16.** `GroomCoatClumpCell` clamps UVs to ±16 before quantising, so strands beyond land in one cell (a root U reaching 11 459 cooked 64 cards carrying 16% of coverage). `GroomLodBuilder` refuses such a groom by name; #1251's clumping has the same limit silently. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
9. **State both anti-thrash bounds.** `Hysteresis` slides thresholds, so a dead band of `2h x threshold` allows at most one transition for narrower camera travel; `HoldFrames` requires that many consecutive stable frames to coarsen. Refining is always immediate. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
10. **Keep LOD state in `Scene`, keyed by UUID, never in the pass.** A pass runs once per camera, hysteresis must advance once per frame (split-screen would burn the hold twice as fast). [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
11. **Give the three budgets (visibility, simulation, shadow) three curves and three stability counters.** A shared counter recouples them through hysteresis. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
12. **Add `GroomLodPolicy::Shadow`'s step to `GroomCoatShadow::SelectCoatLodStep`'s answer;** the shadow axis is a bias on resolution, not a budget. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
13. **Include the representation in the geometry cache key.** A card level and the base groom hash the same at the same budget; otherwise the hand-over serves old strand geometry. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
14. **Do not ship the shell tier.** The highest solid fraction measured was 0.064 (three coats, six distances to 4 px). `GroomLodFallbackReason::MeshTierNotSelected` reports it; `GroomLodShellTier.TheShellTierIsMeasuredBeforeItIsRefused` failing means a coat saturates, so revisit rather than hard-code. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-rules)
15. **Keep the LOD types padding-free.** `GroomLodComponent`, `GroomLodPolicy`, `GroomLodBudgetCurve`, `GroomLodDecision`, `GroomLodState` use `Math::BitwiseEqual(*this, other)` for `operator==`: order members 4-byte then 1-byte, name the tail explicitly (#1019), and list them in `BitwiseEqualLayoutTest`. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#sizeofgroomlodcomponent-and-the-other-pinned-layouts)
16. **Re-import every `.ologroom` written before version 3.** `MinSupportedVersion` moves with `CurrentVersion` (`binary-format-versioning.md`); `GroomLodRoundTripTest` pins the readable version error. Committed grooms under `OloEditor/assets/Grooms/` were regenerated. [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#the-version-bump-costs-every-cooked-groom-on-disk)
17. **Diagnose a wrong LOD by its counter.** Stays strand tier: `GroomLodFallbackReason::LevelNotCooked` (re-import). Cards instead of shell: `MeshTierNotSelected`. Flicker: `GroomLodStats::RepresentationChanges` near groom count. Thin at range: `GroomsAtCompensationCap` non-zero / `MaxWidthCompensation` at cap. Detaches past hand-over: stale source map (rule 7). Rebuild every frame: sliding budget (rule 3). [Details](groom-representation-lod-details.md#what-a-wrong-lod-looks-like-and-which-counter-names-it)

Measured numbers are asserted in `GroomLodComparisonTest` and described in `docs/analysis/groom-representation-lod-1252.md`.
