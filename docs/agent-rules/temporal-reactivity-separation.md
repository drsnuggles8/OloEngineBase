# Temporal reactivity: separate the causes, and give the coverage term a dead band

Covers the shared history model (`Renderer/SurfaceHistory.h`: `TemporalReactivity`, `EvaluateTemporalReactivity`; GLSL twin `OloEditor/assets/shaders/include/SurfaceHistory.glsl`) and the sequence instruments in `Renderer/TemporalSequenceMetrics.h` (issue #1256). Read before changing TAA/history rejection, the G-Buffer RT3 channel, or measuring temporal quality. Before pointing the instruments at anything, read [temporal-measurement-control-arms.md](temporal-measurement-control-arms.md).

## Rules

1. **Keep the causes of history rejection apart.** Surface motion, coverage change and material/profile change are carried as three scalars, not one blended confidence; one number cannot be tuned per subject or attributed. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#why-one-confidence-number-is-not-enough)
2. **Give a reactive term driven by a stochastic estimator a dead band sized to that estimator's own per-frame noise.** Without it the feature makes hair worse than off while still captures look fine. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#the-dead-band-is-the-load-bearing-part)
3. **Size the band to the worst consecutive delta, not the amplitude.** Two frames of zero-mean `±A` jitter differ by `2A`; for a `±0.06` estimator a `0.08` band gives shimmer ratio 1.15 versus the no-band control, `0.13` gives 0.315. Shipped `CoverageNoiseDeadBand` default is `0.12`; a noisier estimator needs it raised. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#the-dead-band-is-the-load-bearing-part)
4. **For a real stochastic coat compare neighbourhood means, not per-pixel coverage.** No per-pixel band absorbs a strand winning or losing a pixel; see [temporal-coverage-means-not-samples.md](temporal-coverage-means-not-samples.md). [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#the-dead-band-is-the-load-bearing-part)
5. **Pin dead-band behaviour with its control.** `TemporalReconstructionSequenceTest.StochasticCoverageNoiseMustNotDriveTheReactiveTerm` asserts the no-dead-band control actually shimmers first; a control that does not move means a broken test. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#the-dead-band-is-the-load-bearing-part)
6. **Combine the causes by multiplying surviving fractions, `(1-m)(1-c)(1-p)`.** Not a max (masks the other causes) and not a sum (saturates past 1, clamp turns three small causes into total rejection). [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#temporal-reactivity-separate-the-causes-and-give-the-coverage-term-a-dead-band)
7. **Keep the graded reactive term and the hard `CoverageMismatch` rejection both.** The term saturates at a change of 0.35 (confidence 0, history length kept); the rejection at 0.5 makes `AccumulateTemporalMoments` reset `HistoryLength` to 1 and reseed. Collapsing them makes a brief thinning discard a long convergence. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#a-graded-term-and-a-hard-rejection-are-not-two-ways-of-saying-the-same-thing)
8. **Run the negative control; when something survives it, that is a gap in the tests.** Disabling the rejection changed no assertion because the reactive term defended the LOD reproduction. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#a-graded-term-and-a-hard-rejection-are-not-two-ways-of-saying-the-same-thing)
9. **Measure temporal defects across frames, with one instrument per defect.** `MeasureShimmer` (static configuration), `MeasureGhosting` (a step to a new steady state), `MeasureDetail` (retained variance against an unfiltered reference); never collapse them into one number, since a blur-to-flat resolve beats shimmer and ghosting. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#measure-across-frames-or-do-not-measure)
10. **Scan `MeasureGhosting` backwards and exclude untouched pixels.** Forward scan reports an overshooting history as settled on the dip; pixels neither side touches are excluded from every metric (as `GroomCoverage::CompareCoverage`). [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#measure-across-frames-or-do-not-measure)
11. **Run temporal measurements headless under mock time with an in-run control arm.** A live pixel A/B cannot answer on a moving subject (69% of pixels move between two captures of the same windy scene). [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#measure-across-frames-or-do-not-measure)
12. **When adding a field to `SurfaceHistoryRecord`, visit every GLSL factory that assigns fields individually.** An unassigned GLSL struct field is undefined, not zero. Factories: `PostProcess_SSGIResolve.glsl`, `RayTracedShadowResolve.glsl`, `ReSTIR_DI_TemporalReuse.glsl`, `ReSTIR_GI_TemporalReuse.glsl`. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#adding-a-field-to-the-shared-record)
13. **Default the new C++ field so existing callers are unaffected, and gate it behind a `TestMask` bit.** Coverage defaults to 1.0 so an opaque caller's delta is zero; the finiteness check and rejection are both opt-in. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#adding-a-field-to-the-shared-record)
14. **Assert the GPU result against the C++ model on the same inputs, never a constant.** Extend `tests/ShaderUnit_SurfaceHistory.glsl`; constants let a retune drift the two implementations apart. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#adding-a-field-to-the-shared-record)
15. **Write four channels for every velocity/RT3 write.** G-Buffer RT3 is `RGBA16F`: `.rg` velocity, `.b` coverage, `.a` material profile; everything else writes the opaque default `vec4(velocity, 1.0, 0.0)`. `GBufferCoverageChannelContractTest` scans shaders for a velocity write that does not cover four channels. Snow-capable writers put snow weight in `.a` (#1451). [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#where-the-coverage-signal-comes-from)
16. **Write each subject's own coverage quantity into `.b`.** Groom: widened strand alpha (stochastic coat marks `.a` with `OLO_STOCHASTIC_COVERAGE_MARK`, -1, #1552); near foliage: cutout alpha x LOD fade; impostor foliage: `card.Coverage * card.DistFade`. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#where-the-coverage-signal-comes-from)
17. **Do not exclude RT3 from the MSAA resolve.** Averaging coverage with the cleared 0 is the sub-pixel measurement; coverage range widens with sample count. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#msaa-sharpens-the-coverage-signal-rather-than-damaging-it)
18. **Confirm MSAA applied before trusting a sweep.** The renderer-settings tool acks an `msaa` change without necessarily applying it; GPU cost scaling (4 to 11 ms across 1x to 8x) is the evidence. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#msaa-sharpens-the-coverage-signal-rather-than-damaging-it)
19. **Keep TAA's `MotionMaxReactivity = 0`.** TAA already scales feedback by motion through `OloTemporalMotionFeedback`; feeding the motion term into `confidence` too squares the motion ramp. The history plane is extracted from the velocity target, so TAA stays single-attachment. [Details](temporal-reactivity-separation-details.md#the-consumer-and-the-double-counting-trap)
