# Every lit-surface term has one owner, and every signal says what it stores

Issue #1336. Read before adding or changing any lighting term, ambient/indirect technique, AO or shadow multiplication, or a buffer that carries light. Code: `Renderer/LightingSignalContract.h`, `include/PBRCommon.glsl` (`oloComposeReflectedLighting`, `oloComposeSurfaceRadiance`, `oloNormalizedIrradiance`), `include/AmbientLadder.glsl`. Tests: `LightingSignalContractTest`, `LightingSignalContractGpuTest`, `LightingSignalCompositionEvidenceTest`.

## Rules

1. **Give every term one owner, a light partition, or a confidence mixture; never two additive estimates.** A second estimator replaces the first over the part it answers for. A double count looks like plausible light and a still frame cannot show it. Ownership is resolved by `ResolveLightingSignalOwnership`; `LightingSignalContractTest` walks every path and live technique. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#the-rules)
2. **Name the quantity at every hand-off, from `LightingSignalKind`.** Radiance *L*; normalized irradiance *E/pi*; irradiance *E*; contribution (*f.L.cos*); visibility; confidence. *E* becomes *E/pi* in exactly one place, `oloNormalizedIrradiance`; document what a buffer stores in its producer's header comment. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#what-each-signal-stores)
3. **Multiply a visibility only into the term it was computed for.** Material and screen-space AO go on ambient; a light's shadow map, RT mask, cloud shadow and contact shadow go on that light; emission is never occluded; a traced tier (ReSTIR GI or PT) is never multiplied by AO. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#the-rules)
4. **Compose raster paths through `oloComposeReflectedLighting` then `oloComposeSurfaceRadiance`.** Do not keep a private copy of the ambient ladder; call `evaluateAmbientLadderSplitEx`. Forward terrain, voxel terrain and foliage use the shared ladder via `u_TerrainAmbientLadder` and `u_LeafIds.w`. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#what-1336-found-and-fixed)
5. **Convert *E* to *E/pi* for lightmap and DDGI/probe volume values before the ladder.** The ladder computes `kD.X.albedo`, Lambertian only for *X = E/pi*; the IBL cube stores *E/pi*, lightmaps (atlas, RT5) and probe volume store *E*. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#what-1336-found-and-fixed)
6. **Do not let ReSTIR GI own specular, and keep ReSTIR PT outside the diffuse split.** GI radiance is contribution, diffuse lobe only (no AO); PT is both lobes, added outside the split so diffusion does not blur its specular. Hybrid tiers own nothing without a G-Buffer. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#what-each-signal-stores)
7. **Make skin pixels decline ReSTIR DI and keep the light loop.** DI has no skin lobe, tint or diffusion. DI stands down on `ReSTIRDIFallbackReason::LightsBeyondShaderBound`; emissive triangles past the encodable index are counted in `EmittersBeyondEncodableIndex`. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#what-1336-found-and-fixed)
8. **Make SSGI a signed delta against the ladder, not an addition.** It re-selects the ladder's diffuse rung at the receiver with the same function and AO: `kD.albedo.Sum(w).L/N - (Sum(w)/N).ladder`; a miss contributes nothing. The furnace case in `LightingSignalCompositionEvidenceTest` pins it. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#what-1336-found-and-fixed)
9. **Apply contact shadow inside the light loop to Lights[0]'s visibility, and AO to the ambient split in DeferredLighting.** The post passes (`AOApply`, contact shadow) keep only the debug view. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#what-1336-found-and-fixed)
10. **Keep Forward/Deferred parity.** Forward and ray-tier surfaces take the deferred roughness floor (ray tiers its wetness too); RT-reflection sun term has 1/pi; ReSTIR PT sky is `uniform + cube`; RT shadows must not write 0 for back-facing pixels (erases transmission); forward terrain AO must not darken direct light. See [forward-deferred-parity-measurement.md](forward-deferred-parity-measurement.md). [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#what-1336-found-and-fixed)
11. **Keep the groom environment term single-converted.** The cube is already *E/pi*; dividing again by pi (#1450) made coats pi too dark, and the sky's IBL intensity must apply. `GroomEnvironmentFurnaceTest` pins both. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#fixed-since)
12. **Do not change legacy split-sum specular (`F_roughness.A + B`).** It moves every Legacy pixel; ClosureV2 is the corrected closure (ADR 0016). Raster samples the mip chain, the reference tracer level 0 (ADR 0022). [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#declared-approximations-kept-on-purpose)
13. **Treat #1325 as open.** RT reflections and SSR still `mix()` over the whole lit colour; replacing only specular needs the old specular as its own signal. Media (fog, volumetrics) applies after surface composition, reflections included. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#owned-elsewhere-or-counted)
14. **Apply forward screen-space AO to the ambient term inside the composing shader (#1452).** `SelectScreenSpaceAOApplication` answers `AmbientTermInLighting` on every path; `PostProcess_SSAOApply` runs only for the AO debug view. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#forward-screen-space-ao-1452)
15. **Keep the forward graph order: `ScenePrepassPass`, `GPUDrivenOcclusionPrepassPass`, `FoliagePrepassPass` (#1474), SSAO or GTAO and sphere proxies, then ScenePass colour.** With AO live, force the prepass on; it writes the view normal to scene attachment 2 through `DepthNormalPrepass*.glsl` using `include/ForwardShadingNormal.glsl`. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#forward-screen-space-ao-1452)
16. **Let a surface read AO only if the prepass drew it.** PBR (static and skinned), terrain, voxel terrain and foliage do; blended PBR (`u_AlphaMode == 2`), water and groom strands take none (on Deferred too), and unlit writers (skybox, light cubes, grid, particles, decals, fluid) apply nothing. `CommandDispatch` publishes AO and `ForwardAODepth` at `TEX_SSAO` / `TEX_POSTPROCESS_DEPTH` and ScenePass republishes right before colour draws (Forward+ culling rebinds slot 19); `ScreenSpaceAOParams` starts every frame not live; a mirrored replay suspends them. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#forward-screen-space-ao-1452)
17. **Make a prepass twin carve its colour program's exact coverage.** Foliage twins (`Foliage_Instance_DepthNormal`, `Foliage_Impostor_DepthNormal`) include the colour program's vertex stage (`invariant gl_Position`) and repeat its discards exactly, not the G-Buffer twin's 0.3 cut or density dither. `AOFoliageParityTest` catches pixels brightening when AO turns on. [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#forward-screen-space-ao-1452)
18. **Treat snow as a material layer, defined only in `include/SnowLayer.glsl`.** The covered fraction blends albedo, roughness, metallic, AO and emission before lighting; sparkle is a directional-light specular lobe gated by that light's visibility; subsurface hands `(diffuse, -weight)` to scene attachment 4 and `SSSPass` adds `strength * (blur - diffuse)`. Deferred carries the weight in G-Buffer RT3.a and the snow-filled normal in RT1. Scene alpha is not a snow channel (#1451). [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#snow-is-a-material-layer-1451)
19. **Add a new technique in four steps.** Add it to `LightingEstimator` and `ResolveLightingSignalOwnership`; document what its buffer stores; compose via the two PBRCommon functions or state why not; add an enable/disable case where the correct answer is zero change (white furnace, emission-only scene, or no light source for the term). [Details](lighting-signal-contract-details.md#how-to-add-a-technique)
