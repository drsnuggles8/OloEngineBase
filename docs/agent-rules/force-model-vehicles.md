# Force-model vehicles (boats, aircraft) on top of Jolt

Rules for adding a **force-model** vehicle (submarine, hovercraft, helicopter, balloon, sail): anything driven by `AddForce`/`AddTorque` on a plain dynamic rigidbody instead of a Jolt constraint. Every failure here leaves the suite green (issues #438, #897, #899).

## Rules

1. **Wire the vehicle into both physics call sites.** Forces must be queued before the world step. Runtime/Play: register a gameplay-scheduler node with `.Writes(kBodyForces).Before("PhysicsKick")`. Editor Simulate: a direct call at the top of `Scene::StepPhysics`, next to `FluidSystem::OnUpdate`. Miss one and the vehicle is inert in that mode. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#2-both-physics-call-sites-or-the-feature-only-works-in-one-editor-mode)
2. **Use `Scene::GetSimulationTime()` as the wave/field clock, never `Time::GetTime()`.** That keeps the vehicle reproducible across frame pacings and rollback re-sim (issue #452). [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#2-both-physics-call-sites-or-the-feature-only-works-in-one-editor-mode)
3. **Choose forces over a Jolt constraint when the vehicle rides a continuous medium.** Use forces for buoyancy, lift and drag, when the vehicle stays an ordinary rigid body, and when it composes by summation with `BuoyancySystem`, `ClothWindSystem`, `FluidSystem`. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#1-constraint-or-forces-pick-by-what-the-vehicle-rides-on)
4. **Name direction vectors for where they point, and derive control signs from a cross product.** Vehicles are `+Z` forward, `+Y` up, so starboard is local `-X` (`right == forward x up`). Use `EntityFacing::LocalStarboard(ForwardConvention::PlusZ)` (`Scene/EntityFacing.h`). Signs: bow to starboard = negative about up; roll to starboard = positive about `+Z`; nose up = positive about starboard `-X`. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#11-fix-the-axis-convention-before-the-first-sign-issue-897)
5. **Test direction with a signed world-space displacement against an unsteered baseline, never a rate or `abs()`.** `BoatTest.StarboardHelmMovesTheBoatToStarboardNotToPort` is the model; keep the window short enough that the hull cannot swing past 90 degrees and assert that bound. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#11-fix-the-axis-convention-before-the-first-sign-issue-897)
6. **When an axis convention bug is found, grep every reader of that axis.** `rightFlat` stayed wrong because hull drag is symmetric; only the asymmetric rudder (`m_LeftRightSplit`) exposed it. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#11-fix-the-axis-convention-before-the-first-sign-issue-897)
7. **Sample the medium where the vehicle touches it, through `Physics3D/WaterProbe`.** Propeller thrust samples at the drive point (`m_ThrustOffsetY/Z`); hull drag samples at the keel, from `BuoyancyComponent::m_ProbeExtents.y` when present. Do not re-resolve water tiles locally. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#3-sample-the-medium-where-the-vehicle-actually-touches-it)
8. **Add static stability, not only rate damping.** Rate damping `torque -= axis * (dot(angVel, axis) * coefficient * mass)` is neutrally stable. Add the weathervane term `torque += cross(forward, velDir) * (k * q * wingArea)`; it is perpendicular to `forward` so it cannot fight ailerons. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#4-rate-damping-is-not-stability)
9. **Keep the aerodynamic invariants.** Scale control torques by `clamp(airspeed / authoritySpeed, 0, 1)`; give the lift curve a post-stall falloff; include induced drag (`Cd = Cd0 + k*Cl^2`); lift direction is `normalize(cross(starboardWing, velDir))`, skipped when degenerate. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#4-rate-damping-is-not-stability)
10. **Decide per vehicle whether thrust is projected onto the horizontal plane.** `BoatSystem` projects the hull forward axis horizontally and bails out when degenerate; an aircraft thrusts along the true nose. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#5-project-thrust-into-the-plane-the-vehicle-actually-travels-in)
11. **Keep `mEngineTorqueRatio` summing to 1 and `mLimitedSlipRatio` strictly above 1.** Derive the second ratio as `1 - frontSplit`. Floor slip ratios at `1.001f`, never `1.0f` (`JPH_ASSERT(mLimitedSlipRatio > 1.0f)` crashes Debug builds), and pin it with `EXPECT_GT(..., 1.0f)`. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#6-jolt-vehicledifferentialsettings-gotchas)
12. **Guard the drive-mode enum against out-of-range values.** A corrupt save or raw script write must fall back to a valid mode, not index off the wheel array. A wheel may steer and be driven at once, so front-wheel drive only re-indexes the differential to wheels 0/1. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#6-jolt-vehicledifferentialsettings-gotchas)
13. **Test both contract and behaviour.** Contract: read the drivetrain via `JoltScene::GetVehicleDrivetrain` and assert wheel indices and ratios (catches the wrong axle). Behaviour: assert the chassis moves by metres. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#7-testing-contract-and-behaviour-because-neither-alone-is-enough)
14. **Build the discriminator into every force-model scenario.** Pair each test with its baseline (unpowered boat drifts < 0.25 m; disabled aircraft free-falls ~78 m in 4 s), test the negative case (boat over dry land, stationary aircraft, stationary rudder), assert the angular rate collapses after stick release plus one 60 s hands-off flight, and re-test rotated. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#7-testing-contract-and-behaviour-because-neither-alone-is-enough)
15. **Verify the vehicle in a running frame.** `VehiclesTest.olo` authors driver inputs in YAML. Set `OLO_MCP_ALLOW_WRITES=1` before `attach` to unlock `olo_scene_open`/`olo_scene_play`. Play renders from the runtime `CameraComponent`, so `olo_screenshot` refuses a `camera` pose there; read poses via `olo_scene_get_entity`. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#8-still-verify-it-in-a-running-frame)
16. **Aim cameras by computation and put them on the track line.** Forward is `rot * (0,0,-1)`; for look direction `d`: `pitch = asin(d.y)`, `yaw = atan2(-d.x, -d.z)`. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#8-still-verify-it-in-a-running-frame)
17. **Author box colliders in unit-mesh half-extents.** Box colliders are multiplied by the transform scale (`JoltShapes::ApplyScaleToBoxExtents`) and primitives are unit-sized, so a collider matching a scaled cube is `HalfExtents: [0.5, 0.5, 0.5]`. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#8-still-verify-it-in-a-running-frame)
18. **Model landing gear as contact points; do not add pitch authority.** `AircraftComponent::m_HasLandingGear` enables three sprung raycast legs, forces applied at each contact point, mains aft of the CoM at `m_MainGearOffsetZ`. Keep `m_GearLength` above the fuselage collider half-height, express `m_GearStiffness` as a multiple of weight, split rolling and lateral friction, keep gear opt-in, prove it with a gear-on/off pair. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#9-ground-contact-put-the-pivot-where-the-gear-is)
19. **Round-trip test every scene-serialized write-once field.** `Rigidbody3DComponent::m_InitialLinearVelocity`/`m_InitialAngularVelocity` (applied in `JoltBody::SetupCreatedBody`) default to zero so old scenes load unchanged and are clamped on read. A dropped field still simulates from rest, so pin it with a round-trip test (`ComponentRoundTrip.Rigidbody3DWithoutInitialVelocityKeysLoadsAtRest`). [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#10-a-scene-must-be-able-to-author-a-moving-body)
20. **For wind-driven vehicles, force depends on apparent velocity.** `apparent = trueWind - hullVelocity`; skipping the subtraction makes the vehicle accelerate without bound downwind. Test by moving the vehicle downwind and re-reading apparent speed. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#the-force-depends-on-apparent-velocity-so-it-is-self-limiting-downwind)
21. **Solve a control surface's angle in closed form.** For a flat sail, `phi = atan2(b, a) / 2` with `a = wind.forward`, `b = wind.port`; clamping it into the travel limit gives the constrained optimum. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#solve-the-control-surfaces-angle-in-closed-form-dont-search-it)
22. **Treat the travel limit as both the no-go zone and the speed governor.** Raising it to point higher also raises speed; do not add a separate speed clamp. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#the-travel-limit-is-the-no-go-zone-and-the-speed-governor-and-that-is-a-design-lever)
23. **Auto-trim holds its angle once achievable drive is not positive.** Chasing the head-to-wind optimum slams the control across as the apparent wind flickers sides. The vehicle still receives the negative force. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#auto-trim-must-stop-chasing-when-nothing-can-be-reached)
24. **Measure a vehicle with a steering controller, a big water surface, an upright assertion and a fresh instance per leg.** Use a PD controller on heading error, an analytic surface large enough for the window plus a still-floating assertion, `(rot * up).y > 0.5` rather than height alone, and a freshly spawned vehicle (disabled until its turn) per measurement; resetting rotation does not reach the transform. [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#measuring-one-of-these-in-a-test-three-ways-0-ms-lied-issue-899)
25. **Pin tuning ratios that span components by test.** Sail area (`SailComponent`), hull forward drag (`BoatComponent`) and `BuoyancyComponent::m_LinearDrag` (applied to the whole velocity vector) form one ratio. Pin it with the shipped `DriftSailingTest` cases (neither becalmed nor uncapped). [Details](force-model-vehicles-details.md#and-the-tuning-is-a-ratio-spread-across-components-which-is-how-it-rots)
